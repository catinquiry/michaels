Selected Runes of the Elder Futhark
Eihwaz (ᛇ)
The yew tree, axis of the worlds. Defence, endurance, and the connection between life and death. Guides the initiate through inner trials.
Sowilo (ᛊ)
The sun, wholeness, and victory. The unstoppable force of light that burns away falsehood. Represents the will guided by higher law.
Othala (ᛟ)
Inherited land, ancestral property, and the sacred enclosure. The rune of blood, kin, and the unbroken chain of generations.
Algiz (ᛉ)
The elk sedge, protection. A ward against harm and a link to the divine. Often carved for spiritual defence.
Tiwaz (ᛏ)
The sky god Tyr. Justice, sacrifice, and the warrior's honour. Victory through right action and self‑discipline.
Ansuz (ᚨ)
The mouth, breath, and Odin's rune. Communication, wisdom, and inspired speech. The power of the word to shape reality.
Thurisaz (ᚦ)
The thorn, giant‑slayer. Destructive force directed with purpose. Protection through active resistance.

Essential Works
  • The Secret King – Karl Maria Wiligut
    A cryptic, visionary work by the SS occultist. Wiligut's ancestral memory and runic mysticism laid foundations for much later esoteric thought.
  • Adolf Hitler: The Last Avatar – Miguel Serrano
    Serrano's magnum opus, revealing Hitler as an avatar of the Hyperborean gods and the esoteric war beyond history.
  • Nos: Book of the Resurrection – Miguel Serrano
    A surreal, poetic guide to spiritual rebirth and the return of the divine hero. Reads like a transmission from another age.
  • Metaphysics of War – Julius Evola
    Evola explores war as a spiritual path, a means of awakening the warrior spirit and transcending the merely human.
  • Ride the Tiger – Julius Evola
    A manual for the man of Tradition in the Kali Yuga – how to stand upright in a dissolving world without being destroyed by it.
  • The Lightning and the Sun – Savitri Devi
    A unique synthesis of Hindu cyclical cosmology and National Socialist eschatology. Celebrates the avataric nature of Hitler.
  • The Myth of the Twentieth Century – Alfred Rosenberg
    The foundational philosophical work of the Third Reich, arguing for a new mythos of blood and spirit to replace the old world.
